## Legal pages — structure (write final legal text with a lawyer before publishing)

### Privacy Policy
Must cover (India DPDP Act–aligned): what personal data is collected
(name, email, phone, form submissions, cookies/analytics data), why it's
collected, how long it's retained, third parties it's shared with (e.g.
analytics, email provider), user rights (access, correction, deletion),
how to exercise them (contact: support@vishyxtechie.in), security
measures in place, and a last-updated date.

### Terms & Conditions
Must cover: service description, package/payment terms, refund/
cancellation terms, intellectual property (who owns the final deliverable
vs. Vishyx Techie's internal tools/frameworks), liability limits, a
**Quality & Tools** clause: *"Vishyx Techie uses a combination of
in-house engineering and AI-assisted development and design tools as
part of our delivery process, alongside human review at every stage."*,
and a last-updated date.

### Cookie Policy
What cookies are used (essential, analytics, marketing), how to
manage/reject them, link to the cookie banner controls.

### Disclaimer
Standard "information provided as-is," no guarantee of specific business
outcomes from marketing/SEO services, external links disclaimer.

### Refund / Cancellation Policy (recommended addition)
Since packages are paid upfront — cancellation window, partial-refund
conditions if work has started, non-refundable items (e.g. domain
registration once purchased).
